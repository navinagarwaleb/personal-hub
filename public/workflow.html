Book Lab Pipeline

Six Hermes skills that turn a story idea into a print-ready illustrated children's book. Each skill is an agent workflow with its own role in the assembly line.

Legend Ideation Review Illustration Rendering Inspection Shipping Data flow →

How each skill works

1

Story Ideator

Skill: story-ideator

Clarifies intent, generates 3 India-centric children's book concepts with deity companions. User picks one.

↓
2

Story Auditor

Skill: story-auditor

Production, safety, AI illustration, and personality audit. Catches issues before illustration begins.

↓
3

Scene Generator

Skill: scene-generator

Converts the approved story into 14 structured illustrated spreads (spreads 03–16) with page 28 as the closing name-game note.

↓
4

3D Illustrator Generator

Skill: 3d-illustration-generator-vibrant

Transforms spread data into enterprise-grade 3D illustration prompts with vibrant Pixar-quality art direction.

↓
5

Prompt Auditor

Skill: prompt-auditor

Technical QA inspector for AI image prompt rows. Validates structure, style, hero insertion, and safety.

↓
6

Book Factory

Skill: book-factory

Runs the full pipeline from ideation to export. Produces Google Sheet outputs and print-ready assets.
